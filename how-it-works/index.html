How it works

How live interview assist works in four steps

From access request to speakable answers: attach your resume, share your interview tab in Chrome for Teams, Meet, or Zoom in the browser, and respond with confidence.

  1. 01

    Log in to the live app

    Use interview.ashitconsulting.ca to create your account or sign in.

  2. 02

    Attach resume + role context

    Add resume, optional job description, and answer preferences. Setup saves to your account.

  3. 03

    Share your Chrome tab

    Start live assistance, share the interview tab in Chrome, turn on tab audio, and use it with Teams, Meet, or Zoom in the browser.

  4. 04

    Speak from suggested answers

    When a question is detected, InTheFinals drafts a first-person answer you can read naturally.
