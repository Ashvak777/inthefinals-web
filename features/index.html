Features

Live interview assist features that matter under pressure

InTheFinals helps you share your interview tab, get resume-grounded suggested answers, and speak clearly under pressure — especially in Power BI, data, SQL, cloud, and IT rounds.

Share your Chrome tab

Share the interview tab in Chrome or Edge (with tab audio on) so InTheFinals can hear the interviewer — not your mic.

Resume-grounded answers

Answers stay tied to your uploaded experience. No invented employers, metrics, or fake stories.

Short / Medium / Long

Choose speakable length. Medium and Long can include compact SQL/DAX/code when needed.

Depth, voice, examples

Optional controls for balanced vs more technical answers, delivery voice, and closing with a resume example.

Cloud resume setup

Your resume, job description, and preferences sync to your account across devices.

Admin-ready operations

Built for ASH-operated cohorts with usage visibility and account controls.
