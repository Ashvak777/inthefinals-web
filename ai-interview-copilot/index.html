Live interview help

AI interview copilot for live calls

InTheFinals is an AI interview copilot that listens to your shared Chrome or Edge call tab, transcribes the interviewer, and suggests resume-grounded answers you can speak in real time — not generic chatbot scripts.

How it works

Three steps from setup to speakable answers.

01

Add resume + role

Upload your resume and optional job description so the copilot stays tied to your real experience.

02

Share the call tab

In Chrome or Edge, share the Zoom, Meet, or Teams tab with tab audio on and start listening.

03

Use resume-grounded answers

When a question lands, use Short, Medium, or Long suggestions while you speak. Medium and Long can include compact technical detail when needed.

Who it suits

Candidates who want a live interview copilot for technical and behavioral rounds — especially Power BI, data, cloud, and IT interviews — and prefer answers grounded in their own resume over generic scripts.

FAQ

What is an AI interview copilot?

InTheFinals is an AI interview copilot that listens to your shared Chrome or Edge call tab, transcribes interviewer speech, and suggests concise answers grounded in your resume and job description. Try it at app.inthefinals.ai or email info@inthefinals.ai.

How is this different from ChatGPT?

InTheFinals is built for the live call: it listens to the shared interview tab and returns speakable, resume-grounded suggestions during the round — not a separate chat you have to paste questions into.

Does it invent project stories?

No. Answers are meant to stay grounded in the resume and job context you provide. You remain responsible for what you say in the interview.

How do I start?

Create a free account at app.inthefinals.ai/signup. Two free live sessions, then CA$14.99/month for 7 sessions.
