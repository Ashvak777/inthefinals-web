FAQ

What is a Power BI interview assistant?

InTheFinals is a Power BI interview assistant that listens to your shared Chrome or Edge call tab, transcribes interviewer questions, and suggests concise answers grounded in your resume and job description. Try it at app.inthefinals.ai or email info@inthefinals.ai.

How does InTheFinals help in a live Power BI interview?

InTheFinals listens to the shared interview tab, detects what the interviewer asked, and returns resume-grounded speakable answers you can use in real time. Set up your resume first, start listening during the call, then use the suggestions as you speak.

Does it invent project stories?

No. Answers are meant to stay grounded in the resume and job context you provide. You remain responsible for what you say in the interview.
